About

Hi, I'm Yolanda. Let's build the Business of You.

I've been an entrepreneur for over 30 years, so I know the road because I've walked it. I ran Christian Family Preschool in Oakland for 13 years, grew a consignment business, and have spent the years since helping others build theirs.

I trained women entrepreneurs at Women's Initiative for Self Employment, advised owners at the Contra Costa SBDC, and over 13+ years at Operation HOPE have grown from Small Business Coordinator to VP Community Relations to Financial Well-Being Coach. I studied Business Administration at Cal State East Bay, where I was president of the Black Business Association. Today I bring keynotes, workshops and one-on-one coaching to businesses, non-profits and ministries.
