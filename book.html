Chapter Previews

Chapter 1: The Identity Reset

I was 43 years old, lying on a cot in a field hospital in Kandahar Province, when I had the thought that would ultimately change everything. Not about whether I would live or die — I had long made peace with that particular uncertainty. No, the thought was simpler and more terrifying than any enemy I had ever faced.

"I have been living someone else's definition of who I am supposed to be. And I have been doing it my entire life."

That moment of radical honesty — the kind that only comes when life strips away every comfortable lie — became the foundation of everything I now teach. The Identity Reset is not therapy. It is not journaling. It is a surgical extraction of the beliefs, narratives, and labels that were placed on you by other people, and a precise reconstruction of who you actually choose to be...

Chapter 3: Combat-Ready Mindset

In the military, we do not wait to feel ready before we move. Readiness is a discipline, not a feeling. You train until the right response is automatic — until your body and mind react correctly before your conscious brain can hesitate, second-guess, or retreat into fear.

"The entrepreneur who waits to feel confident will be waiting forever. Confidence is built by doing the thing, not before it."

I am going to give you the seven mental protocols that kept me and my soldiers alive across three combat deployments. Each one has a direct application to building a business, leading a team, navigating a crisis, or simply getting out of bed on the days when everything feels impossible...

Chapter 7: The Revenue Command Center

Every mission needs a command center — a place where you can see the entire battlefield at once, make decisions with clear data, and coordinate your forces for maximum impact. Your business is no different. And yet most entrepreneurs run their revenue like they are fumbling around in the dark.

"Revenue is not a number. Revenue is a reflection of how clearly you communicate your value and how consistently you ask to be paid for it."

I am going to walk you through my Revenue Command Center — the exact system I built after my first year in business, when I went from making $47,000 to generating $1.2 million. It is not complicated. It is ruthlessly simple. And that simplicity is exactly why it works...
