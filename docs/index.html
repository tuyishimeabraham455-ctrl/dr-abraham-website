Meet Yo

I know the road, because I've walked it.

I ran Christian Family Preschool in Oakland for 13 years and grew a consignment business on the side. Then I spent my career helping other owners: training women entrepreneurs, advising at the Contra Costa SBDC, and growing from Small Business Coordinator to Financial Well-Being Coach at Operation HOPE.

Today I bring that experience to stages, workshops and one-on-one coaching for businesses, nonprofits and ministries. My promise is simple: we build the business around you, not the other way around.

— Yo

The framework

The Business of You, in five steps.

A clear path from idea to impact. Every step connects to a talk, an episode or a resource.

  1. 01

    Identify your problem

    Great businesses start with a real problem. We name it, size it, and make sure people will pay to solve it.

    Related talk
  2. 02

    Build around your strengths

    Your story, skills and relationships are assets. We design the business around who you already are.

    Related talk
  3. 03

    Fund and protect it

    Credit, capital, trust accounts and the guardrails that keep what you build safe.

    Related talk
  4. 04

    Sustain yourself

    Mental and financial wellness so the founder lasts as long as the business does.

    Related talk
  5. 05

    Scale your voice

    Social capital, partnerships and visibility. Each one, reach one.
