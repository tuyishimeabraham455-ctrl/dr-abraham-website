About

Yolanda "Yo" Butler teaches entrepreneurs how to build a business around themselves, blending business strategy with mental and financial wellness. An entrepreneur for 30+ years, Yo ran Christian Family Preschool in Oakland for 13 years, trained women entrepreneurs, advised owners at the Contra Costa SBDC, and spent 13+ years at Operation HOPE, rising to Financial Well-Being Coach.
