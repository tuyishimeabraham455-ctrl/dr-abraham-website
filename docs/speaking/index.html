FAQ

Questions, answered.

What topics does Yolanda speak on?

Building a business around your strengths, business credit and funding readiness, financial and mental wellness for founders, and the power of social capital and community.

Does she speak virtually?

Yes. Every keynote and workshop can be delivered virtually, and she speaks in person across the San Francisco Bay Area and beyond.

How far ahead should we book?

Most events book two to four months ahead. If your date is sooner, ask anyway; Yo's team will tell you quickly if it can work.

Can a talk be customized for our audience?

Yes. Every talk is tailored after a short planning call about your audience, goals and format.

How are fees set?

Fees depend on the event type, length, location and audience. Yo's team shares a clear quote after a short conversation.
