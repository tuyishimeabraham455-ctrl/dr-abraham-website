Chapter 01

Where I come from.

I grew up in rural Mississippi, the youngest of six children, in a house where dreams felt like a luxury we couldn't afford. Money was always tight and opportunities were scarce. But my mother never let us use that as an excuse. "Abraham," she'd say, "everything is possible, if you're willing to work harder than anyone in the room."

I didn't know it then, but that sentence would become the title of my book, the foundation of my company, and the promise I make to every person I work with.

Chapter 02

Twenty-one years in uniform.

At 18, I enlisted in the United States Army. It wasn't my first choice. It was my only choice. What followed was a 21-year career that took me through three combat deployments, earned me the Bronze Star Medal, and showed me a side of human resilience no classroom could ever teach.

The Army taught me discipline, strategy, and how to lead people when the stakes are as high as they get. Those lessons are the backbone of everything I teach today.

Chapter 03

The turning point.

During my deployment to Afghanistan, I was wounded in action. Lying in a field hospital, alone with my thoughts, I made a decision that changed everything: I would not just survive this. I would use it. Every scar would become a stepping stone. Every battle would become a lesson I could share.

I came home, enrolled on the GI Bill, and earned my PhD in Psychology at USC. I counseled veterans, studied high performance under pressure, and began to see a pattern: the mental frameworks that keep soldiers alive in combat are exactly what entrepreneurs need to thrive.

Chapter 04

The work now.

I launched my coaching practice in 2011 with nothing but a laptop and an unshakeable belief. Today S3 Academy has trained more than 50,000 students in 35 countries, and my clients have generated more than $100 million in new revenue using the Strength, Strategy, Success method.

My book, Everything Is Possible, debuted at #1 on the New York Times bestseller list and is now in 14 languages. I've spoken on Pentagon stages and TEDx stages, in corporate boardrooms and small church basements, because I believe transformation has no zip code.

I'm here to show you that what you dream of is possible, starting right now.
